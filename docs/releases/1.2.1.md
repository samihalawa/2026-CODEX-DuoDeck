# DuoDeck Host 1.2.1

Less to read, more to see.

## What changed

- **See what you're sharing.** While a window streams, the home window shows a live preview of it, refreshed every couple of seconds from the same pictures DuoDeck already takes for your devices. Nothing extra is captured, and nothing shows while the Mac is locked.
- **Fewer words.** The state in one word, your Mac's name, and one short line. Switch descriptions moved into tooltips, and each device is listed once with a plain date.
- Live numbers stay: frames per second, delay and connection beside each device.

Pairing, streaming and settings work as in 1.2.0, and paired devices stay paired.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility from its home window. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…**.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Macs › Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required.

DuoDeck builds on the Apache 2.0 licensed Sill engine. Original attribution and license are retained.
