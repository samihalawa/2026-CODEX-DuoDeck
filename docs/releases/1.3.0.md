# DuoDeck Host 1.3.0

Works with the new DuoDeck for iPhone and iPad.

## What changed

- **Matches the new iPhone and iPad app.** This version and DuoDeck for iPhone and iPad 1.1.0 (build 2026111302) find and pair with each other under the DuoDeck name. Update both together: earlier versions on either side no longer see each other.
- **iPhone Duo back screen.** The iPhone and iPad app can now mirror your Mac on the iPhone Duo's back screen, or turn that screen into a trackpad for your fingers behind the phone. Nothing changes on the Mac.
- Pair your devices again once after updating.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility from its home window. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…**.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required.
