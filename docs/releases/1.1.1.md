# DuoDeck Host 1.1.1

A new look for DuoDeck on the Mac.

## What changed

- A new app icon: a violet upright half over an orange deck, the shape of an iPhone Duo sitting half-folded like a small computer.
- The menu bar icon now uses the same mark, with the same connection indicators as before.
- The icon sits on a white ground instead of blue.

Nothing else changes in this update. Pairing, streaming and settings work as in 1.1.0, and devices you have already paired stay paired.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then finish Screen Recording and Accessibility setup. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…** from the Mac menu bar menu.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Macs › Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or choose **Enter code**, enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**. Start with both devices on the same network.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required for the current native app.

See the [website](https://duodeck.megawebs.com/#downloads) for installation options.
