# DuoDeck Host 1.2.0

A new home window: large, calm and plainly a Mac app.

## What changed

- **A real window.** DuoDeck Host opens to a large window, most of your screen the first time. It can go full screen and remembers its size.
- **What matters, in large type.** The left side says the state in one word (Ready, Streaming) and shows the one thing that matters now: how to connect your first device, the device it is waiting for, or what is streaming with frames per second, delay, round trip and connection as big figures.
- **Controls on the side.** A quiet sidebar holds the picture menu, Fit windows to the device, Sound, Clipboard, Away from home, and your paired devices with when each last connected.
- **No decoration.** Plain window background, standard switches and menus, no gradients or coloured overlays. On macOS 26 the main buttons use Liquid Glass.

Pairing, streaming and settings work as in 1.1.9, and paired devices stay paired.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility from its home window. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…**.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Macs › Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required.
