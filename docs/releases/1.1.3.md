# DuoDeck Host 1.1.3

DuoDeck Host now opens to a home window of its own.

## What changed

- **A home for DuoDeck on your Mac.** Opening DuoDeck Host, clicking its Dock icon or choosing **Open DuoDeck** from the menu bar shows one calm window: whether this Mac is ready, which iPhone or iPad is connected and what it is showing, and the next step. Nobody connected yet? It walks you through it, with an App Store code to scan for the iPhone and iPad app and a **Pair iPhone or iPad…** button.
- **Setup in one place.** Until Screen Recording and Accessibility are both on, the home window lists them with an Allow… button each.
- **Settings stay in Settings.** Every control is still in Settings (⌘,), now titled DuoDeck Settings.
- The Mac app's accent colour now matches the violet of the new icon.

Pairing, streaming and settings work as in 1.1.2, and paired devices stay paired.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility from its home window. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…**.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Macs › Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required.
