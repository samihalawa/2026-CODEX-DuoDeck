# DuoDeck 1.1.0

Control your Mac from your iPhone or iPad with a clearer setup flow and simpler native controls.

## What changed

- A simple paired-D app icon and a plain DuoDeck wordmark, with a cleaner white, grey and blue website.
- A native **Macs** list and **Add Mac** sheet. Scan a code or enter the Mac address and pairing code to connect.
- More room for the picture and trackpad. Keyboard and Shortcuts stay close; an optional shortcut row and extra Mac controls live in settings.
- Saved **Small**, **Medium** and **Full** trackpad sizes for upright layouts, pointer speed and Editing, Browsing, Windows and Code shortcut groups.
- Separate iPhone and iPad settings pages for Trackpad, Display & sound, Connection, Help and Advanced.
- A full Mac app in the Dock and app switcher, with a resizable window, standard minimize, zoom and full-screen controls, and reopening from the Dock. Closing the window keeps your connection running.
- Clear picture-unavailable messages and a retry action when screen capture cannot start, plus recovery when a first video frame arrives before the display is ready.
- Four Mac settings tabs: **General**, **Devices**, **Streaming** and **Advanced**. Setup and pairing are easier to find; network options, permissions and diagnostics are grouped under Advanced.
- Matching **Smooth**, **Balanced** and **Crisp** streaming profiles, plus clearer action labels and help links.
- A simpler website with three setup steps and a clearly labeled interactive browser preview.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then finish Screen Recording and Accessibility setup. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…** from the Mac menu bar menu.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Macs › Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or choose **Enter code**, enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**. Start with both devices on the same network. Choose a window or the desktop and use the trackpad or Keyboard to control it.

The iOS client requires iOS 17 or later. The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. Your Mac must remain awake with DuoDeck Host running. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required for the current native app.

See the [website](https://duodeck.megawebs.com/#downloads) for installation options and the [README](https://github.com/samihalawa/2026-CODEX-DuoDeck#build-the-mac-companion) for source builds.
