# DuoDeck Host 1.1.8

The home window is now a plain Mac window.

## What changed

- **Native design.** The home window uses the same grouped layout, switches and menus as System Settings, in light and dark mode, without custom colours or artwork.
- **What matters, in order.** This Mac and its state at the top; what is streaming and to which device, with live numbers; the switches you change between sessions (picture, fit windows to the device, sound, clipboard, away from home); and your paired devices with when each last connected.
- **Settings** stay one click away in the toolbar, or with ⌘,.

Pairing, streaming and settings work as in 1.1.7, and paired devices stay paired.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility from its home window. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…**.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Macs › Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required.
