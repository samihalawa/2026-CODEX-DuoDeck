# DuoDeck Host 1.1.2

DuoDeck Host now fixes its own permissions.

## What changed

- **Repair Permissions.** If Screen Recording or Accessibility is switched on in System Settings but DuoDeck Host still asks for it, the Permissions page clears the old macOS record and asks again for this copy. Pressing Allow… a second time does the same.
- **One copy of DuoDeck Host.** The Permissions page lists other copies of the app on your Mac and moves them to the Trash in one click, so a switch in System Settings can't belong to a copy you don't use.
- **Move to Applications.** If DuoDeck Host runs from a disk image or Downloads, it offers to move itself to Applications and reopen from there.
- The new app icon from 1.1.1.

Pairing, streaming and settings work as in 1.1.1, and paired devices stay paired.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility from its Permissions page. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…** in DuoDeck Host.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Macs › Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required.
