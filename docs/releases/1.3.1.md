# DuoDeck Host 1.3.1

Mac display resolution scaling, online display detection, and smoother cursor/desktop handoff for DuoDeck on iPhone, iPad, and iPhone Duo.

## What changed

- **Mac display resolution & portrait sizing.** Adds `DisplayResolutionManager` so DuoDeck on iPhone, iPad, and iPhone Duo can switch Mac display resolutions and vertical portrait sizing while streaming.
- **More reliable Desktop switching & cursor movement.** Fixes online display enumeration, mirror wait, automatic Desktop switching, and instant trackpad cursor updates.
- **Compatible with DuoDeck 1.1.0.** Pairs over Wi-Fi or USB with DuoDeck 1.1.0 (including Floating Keyboard and iPhone Duo Back screen modes).

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility from its home window. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…**.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required.
