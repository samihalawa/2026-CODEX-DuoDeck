# DuoDeck Host 1.1.9

The home window gets a look of its own, while staying a Mac app.

## What changed

- **Not a settings page.** The home window now opens with your Mac's state in large type ("Ready", "Streaming"), over a very soft wash of DuoDeck's colours that drifts slowly (and stays still with Reduce Motion).
- **Frosted cards.** Now, Streaming and Paired devices sit on translucent cards with a tinted symbol for each line, and the standard Mac switches and menus inside them. On macOS 26 the main buttons use Liquid Glass.
- Everything from 1.1.8 is still there: what is streaming and to whom with live numbers, the picture menu, Fit windows to the device, Sound, Clipboard, Away from home, and your paired devices.

Pairing, streaming and settings work as in 1.1.8, and paired devices stay paired.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility from its home window. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…**.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Macs › Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required.
