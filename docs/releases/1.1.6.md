# DuoDeck Host 1.1.6

The home window fits what you are doing now.

## What changed

- **Waiting for your iPad.** Once a device is paired, the setup steps and App Store code give way to one short card naming the device DuoDeck is waiting for, with Pair another device… beside it. New Macs still see the full walkthrough.
- **Live connection at a glance.** While streaming, each device shows its frame rate, delay, round trip and connection as clear tags.
- **Quick controls.** Choose Smooth, Balanced or Crisp for the picture, and switch Own display, Sound, Clipboard and Away from home on or off with one click, right in the home window. Each change applies at once, as it does in Settings.
- **Your devices.** Every paired iPhone and iPad is listed, the one connected now first, with when and how each last connected. Manage… opens the full list in Settings.
- **Activity.** The footer opens DuoDeck Host's log in one click, for when something needs a closer look.

Pairing, streaming and settings work as in 1.1.5, and paired devices stay paired.

## Connect your devices

Install DuoDeck Host on a Mac running macOS 14 or later, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility from its home window. Choose **Pair iPhone or iPad…**.

On your iPhone or iPad, open DuoDeck and choose **Macs › Add Mac**. Scan the Mac's code, or enter its address and 12-digit code, then tap **Pair Mac**.

The Mac companion supports Intel and Apple silicon. No DuoDeck account or subscription is required.
