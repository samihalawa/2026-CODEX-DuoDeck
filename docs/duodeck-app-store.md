## Description

```text
Control your Mac from your iPhone or iPad.

DuoDeck shows a Mac window or desktop on your phone or tablet. Use the trackpad to point and click, open the keyboard to type, and keep familiar Mac shortcuts within reach. Your apps keep running on your Mac.

Set up once
Open DuoDeck Host on your Mac and finish the permission steps. In DuoDeck on your iPhone or iPad, open Macs, choose Add Mac, and scan or enter the Mac's pairing code. Choose your paired Mac, then a window or the desktop.

Adjust your trackpad
Choose Small, Medium or Full for the upright layout. Set pointer speed and haptic feedback, and keep your preferences between sessions.

Use familiar shortcuts
Copy, paste, undo, find, switch apps and open Spotlight. Choose Editing, Browsing, Windows or Code shortcuts. Open them when you need them, or turn on the optional shortcut row beside the trackpad.

Keep settings out of the way
Trackpad, Display & sound, Connection, Help and Advanced have their own settings pages. Extra Mac controls are available under Trackpad > More controls. Both apps follow your system's light or dark appearance.

A straightforward Mac companion
DuoDeck Host opens from your Dock with a resizable Mac window. Pair a device, see its connection and choose Smooth, Balanced or Crisp streaming quality. Quick controls are also available from the menu bar. General, Devices, Streaming and Advanced keep setup and everyday settings separate from network options and diagnostics. Audio and clipboard sharing are optional.

What you need
A Mac running macOS 14 or later with DuoDeck Host, and an iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. Start with both devices on the same network. Your Mac must remain awake with DuoDeck Host running. Get the Mac companion at duodeck.megawebs.com/#downloads.

The current native app is free and requires no DuoDeck account or subscription. DuoDeck controls your Mac; it does not add a system-wide pointer to other iPhone apps.

DuoDeck is an independent project based on the Apache 2.0 licensed Sill engine.
```

## App Review instructions

Use these instructions after the exact companion download is published and verified. They describe the real pairing flow and require a reviewer-owned Mac; there is no demo account.

```text
DuoDeck is a remote-control client for the reviewer's own Mac. The Mac runs the apps; DuoDeck displays a window or desktop and sends input back to that Mac. There is no account, login, subscription or in-app purchase to test.

MAC SETUP
Use a Mac with macOS 14 or later, Intel or Apple silicon. Install DuoDeck Host from https://duodeck.megawebs.com/#downloads. Open DuoDeck Host from the Dock and finish the Screen Recording and Accessibility permission steps. Reopen DuoDeck Host if macOS asks you to apply Screen Recording access. Keep the Mac awake. Choose Pair iPhone or iPad... from the DuoDeck menu bar menu to display its pairing code.

IPHONE OR IPAD SETUP
Use iOS 17 or later and put both devices on the same network. Allow Local Network access when asked. Open DuoDeck, then Macs > Add Mac. Choose Scan code and allow Camera access to scan the Mac's code. Alternatively, choose Enter code, enter the Mac address and 12-digit code shown on the Mac, and tap Pair Mac. Camera access is not needed for manual entry. A discovered Mac can also be selected from Macs to open its pairing sheet; that route already knows the Mac address.

WHAT TO TRY
Choose the paired Mac and select a window from Apps or choose Desktop. Point and click with the trackpad. Open Keyboard and type into an editable field on the Mac. Open Shortcuts to try copy, paste, undo or app switching. Hold the device upright and open Settings > Trackpad to try Small, Medium and Full sizes and pointer speed. The optional shortcut row and shortcut group are under Trackpad > Shortcuts. Extra controls are under Trackpad > More controls.

The Mac companion's settings have General, Devices, Streaming and Advanced tabs. General shows setup and pairing, Devices manages paired devices, Streaming contains quality and audio, and Advanced contains network options, remote access, virtual display, permissions and diagnostics. Remote Access is optional and is not needed for the local review flow.

IF THE MAC DOES NOT APPEAR
Confirm that DuoDeck Host is running and the devices share a network. Check Local Network permission on the iPhone or iPad. Use Add Mac > Enter code with the address and code shown by the Mac. Confirm Screen Recording and Accessibility in DuoDeck Host if the picture or input is unavailable.

DuoDeck controls the Mac. It does not provide a system-wide pointer for other iPhone apps. Support: https://github.com/samihalawa/2026-CODEX-DuoDeck/issues. Privacy: https://duodeck.megawebs.com/privacy.
```
