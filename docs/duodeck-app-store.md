## Description

Your Mac. In your hands.

DuoDeck brings Mac windows to your iPhone or iPad, with an adjustable trackpad and useful shortcuts close to your fingers.

MAKE ROOM FOR YOUR WORK
Choose Small, Medium or Full trackpad size. Save your pointer speed, select a shortcut set and keep the controls comfortable. On iPhone Duo, the layout responds to the fold.

EVERYDAY ACTIONS, ONE TAP AWAY
Copy, paste, undo, find, switch apps and open Spotlight. Choose Editing, Browsing, Windows or Code actions. Keyboard and app controls stay within reach while advanced options live in Settings.

A NATIVE COMPANION
DuoDeck Host lives in your Mac menu bar with pairing, permission guidance, device management and Smooth, Balanced and Crisp streaming profiles. Both apps follow the system appearance.

CONNECT YOUR DEVICES
Stream a Mac window or desktop over your local network or a compatible USB connection. Optional audio and clipboard sharing stay under your control. Basic local control requires no DuoDeck account or subscription.

DuoDeck requires a compatible Mac running DuoDeck Host. It controls your Mac and does not provide a system-wide pointer for other iPhone apps. The iOS client requires iOS 17 or later; fold-aware iPhone Duo layouts require iOS 27.1 or later. The Mac companion requires macOS 14 or later.

DuoDeck is an independent project based on the Apache 2.0 licensed Sill engine.
