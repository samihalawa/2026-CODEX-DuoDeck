Good to know

A few answers.

Can I use an ordinary iPhone or iPad?

Yes. DuoDeck supports iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 or later. iPhone Duo gets layouts that respond to the fold on iOS 27.1 or later. Your Mac runs the companion app.

Does it control other iPhone apps?

DuoDeck controls your connected Mac. It is not a system-wide trackpad for other iPhone apps. Your gestures and keyboard commands go to the Mac.

Can I connect away from home?

The Mac companion supports optional Remote Access through your own VPN or network setup. Configure it in Advanced settings and pair your device locally first.

Do I need an account or subscription?

The current native client and host work without a DuoDeck account or subscription. A compatible Mac is required.

Is DuoDeck open source?

Yes. DuoDeck is an independent project built on the Apache 2.0 licensed Sill streaming engine. Source code, license and upstream attribution are available on GitHub.
