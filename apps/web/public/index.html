Connect in three steps.

Install DuoDeck on both devices. Start with your Mac and phone on the same network.

  1. 1

    Open it on your Mac

    Open DuoDeck Host and finish the two permission steps. They let your phone see the screen and send clicks.

    Get the Mac companion →
  2. 2

    Add your Mac on your phone

    Open DuoDeck, choose your Mac, then scan or enter its pairing code. You only need to pair once.

  3. 3

    Choose a window

    Open a Mac app window or the desktop. Use the trackpad to point and click, or tap Keyboard to type.

My Mac isn’t appearing

Make sure DuoDeck Host is running and both devices are on the same local network. Check that Local Network access is allowed for DuoDeck on your iPhone or iPad.

You can also use Add Mac to enter the connection details. On the Mac, open Settings to check permissions and connection status.

Questions and help

Report a problem ↗

Will it work with my iPhone or iPad?

DuoDeck supports iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 or later. The Mac companion requires macOS 14 or later. iPhone Duo also supports fold-aware layouts on iOS 27.1 or later.

Can I use it as a trackpad for other iPhone apps?

DuoDeck controls your connected Mac. It is not a system-wide trackpad for other iPhone apps. Gestures, typing and shortcuts go to the Mac.

Why does the Mac need permissions?

Screen Recording lets DuoDeck display the window you choose. Accessibility lets it deliver your pointer and keyboard actions. The Mac app shows which permission needs attention and opens the relevant system setting.

Does my Mac need to stay on?

Yes. Keep the Mac awake with DuoDeck Host running while you use it from your iPhone or iPad.

Can I connect away from home?

Optional Remote Access works with your own VPN or network setup. Pair locally first, then open Advanced settings on the Mac to configure the connection.

Is DuoDeck open source?

Yes. DuoDeck is an independent project built on the Apache 2.0 licensed Sill streaming engine. The source repository includes the license and original attribution.
