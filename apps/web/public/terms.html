The product

DuoDeck lets a paired iPhone, iPad, or iPhone Duo view and interact with a compatible Mac running DuoDeck Host. A Mac, the companion app, and the permissions required for screen capture and input control are necessary for core operation. DuoDeck is not a system-wide trackpad for unrelated iPhone or iPad apps.

Permitted use

Use DuoDeck only with computers and user accounts you own or have explicit permission to control. You are responsible for managing trusted devices, local-network access, and any optional VPN or remote-access setup.

Open source license

DuoDeck is released under the Apache 2.0 license. The full license, warranty disclaimers, and retained copyright notices are published in the source repository. These terms do not restrict rights granted under Apache 2.0.

Free local mode & availability

Features depend on your operating-system version, hardware, and network connection. Direct local control between your Mac and iPhone/iPad is free and requires no DuoDeck account or paid subscription. Any future optional convenience services would remain optional and never gate local pairing.
