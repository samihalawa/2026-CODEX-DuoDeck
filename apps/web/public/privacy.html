Privacy Policy

Updated 24 November 2026

Your devices, connected directly

DuoDeck connects an iPhone, iPad, or iPhone Duo to DuoDeck Host on your Mac. Screen frames, optional audio, clipboard text, and control input travel between your devices over the connection you configure. The native apps do not use a DuoDeck cloud relay, hosted account database, advertising SDK, or third-party analytics SDK.

Permissions

DuoDeck Host uses macOS Screen Recording to transmit a chosen window or desktop, and Accessibility to deliver pointer and keyboard actions. The iOS app uses Local Network access for discovery and Camera access only when you choose to scan a pairing QR code.

Information kept on your devices

Pairing identities, trusted devices, preferences, custom control decks, and saved connection information are stored locally. The app can retain a thumbnail of your last session for its saved-Mac view. You can remove saved Macs and paired devices inside the apps. Operating-system keychain items can remain after uninstall; removing the app alone does not guarantee every pairing credential is erased.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time, disable clipboard or audio sharing, remove paired devices, and revoke permissions in system settings. Remote Access is optional and uses the network or VPN configuration you supply.

This website

The interactive preview stores your demo layout, shortcut set, and pointer speed in browser local storage. First-party web analytics on our self-hosted PostHog instance (posthog.megawebs.com) remains disabled until you explicitly choose Accept analytics in the consent prompt; you can change or withdraw consent at any time via Privacy preferences in the footer. The server checks GitHub releases for public Mac Host availability without forwarding visitor browser information.

Optional update checks

When DuoDeck Host checks for updates (manually or automatically), it contacts GitHub Releases to check release metadata. GitHub receives the request IP address and app version. Automatic checks can be disabled in General › Updates.

External links and contact

Privacy questions: support@megawebs.com. Product source and issue reporting live in the DuoDeck repository.
